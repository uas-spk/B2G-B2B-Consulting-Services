## F · Pre-submission risk review

- **Refrigerant match** — R-450A (CLIN 0001) and R-290 (0002, 0005) are specified; confirm the offered unit's refrigerant exactly, not merely "low-GWP equivalent".
- **Plug configuration** — CLIN 0004 requires NEMA 5-20P, the others 5-15P; a 5-15P substitution on 0004 is a deviation.
- **Dimensional envelope** — stated dimensions are maxima; oversize is a failure even if capacity matches.
- **Hinge handedness** — CLIN 0002 right-hinged; CLIN 0003 one left / one right. Field-reversible is not the same as supplied configuration unless stated.
- **Country of origin per CLIN** — verify with the manufacturer in writing; a distributor's assurance is not evidence.
- **Amendment watch** — re-check the portal before closing; an amendment obliges a revised offer.
- **Protest route (if needed)** — agency-level program is available and faster than a formal GAO protest; raise issues with the contracting office first.
