Cited determination — complaint found valid
KEVEREST Technologies Inc. v. Shared Services Canada, file PR-2026-001 — complaint filed under subsection 30.11(1) of the Canadian International Trade Tribunal Act, inquiry conducted, complaint prepared and argued personally without counsel. In its determination of 17 August 2026 the Tribunal held that the complaint is valid. It awarded the complainant its reasonable bid-preparation costs, payable by Shared Services Canada (capped by regulation at 2% of the bid price), and its reasonable costs of preparing and proceeding with the complaint — preliminary complexity Level 2, all-inclusive flat rate CAD 2,750.

Read the Tribunal’s determination → A finding that a complaint is valid is the Tribunal’s clearest statement that a procurement went wrong. Public record.
Cited determination — costs awarded and confirmed
Keverest Technologies Inc. v. Department of Public Works and Government Services, file PR-2024-043 — a complaint concerning solicitation WS4279111193, issued by PWGSC on behalf of Transport Canada for an automotive-grade 128-channel LIDAR sensor. Complaint prepared and argued personally, without counsel. In its determination of 29 January 2025 the Tribunal awarded the complainant its reasonable costs of preparing and proceeding with the complaint under section 30.16 of the CITT Act. By order of 13 March 2025 (Randolph W. Heggart, Presiding Member), the Tribunal confirmed its preliminary indications — complexity Level 1, CAD 1,150 — no submissions having contested them, and directed the department to take appropriate action to ensure prompt payment.

Read the Tribunal’s order of 13 March 2025 → A matter of public record. Cited here because the reasoning that persuades a tribunal is the same reasoning that makes a submission difficult to reject in the first place.
